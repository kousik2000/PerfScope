# PerfScope Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 10, 2026

## 1. About PerfScope

PerfScope is a Chrome extension that helps developers inspect webpage performance using available browser APIs. It displays performance metrics and diagnostics to help users investigate loading, rendering, resource usage and related issues.

## 2. Information the extension accesses

Depending on the features used and browser support, PerfScope may access:

- The URL of a webpage being monitored.
- Performance measurements, including available timing metrics, rendering information and resource statistics.
- Monitoring preferences and selected website settings.
- Performance reports and saved snapshots, if supported and enabled.

The extension does not need webpage information unrelated to its performance-monitoring purpose.

## 3. How information is used

Information accessed by PerfScope is used to provide performance monitoring, display diagnostics, generate reports and maintain user-selected settings.

## 5. Permissions

PerfScope may use Chrome permissions such as `activeTab`, `scripting`, `storage` and website access permissions to collect performance measurements, execute its packaged monitoring logic and preserve user preferences.

The exact permissions used are determined by the published extension manifest.

## 6. Data security and retention

Locally stored information may remain in Chrome storage until the user deletes it or removes the extension, subject to Chrome's storage behavior and the extension's retention logic.

Users should avoid sharing exported reports that contain website URLs or other sensitive information.

## 7. Children's privacy

PerfScope is a developer utility and is not designed specifically for children.
